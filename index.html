Executive Conclusion

Data Modeling & Predictive Recommendations

Feature Engineering Priorities

Combine SibSp and Parch into a unified FamilySize metric. Solo passengers had significantly lower survival rates than small families (2-4 members).

Title Extraction from Names

Extract honorifics (Mr, Mrs, Miss, Master) to capture proxy age and marital status interactions, improving predictive classification accuracy in machine learning models.

Missing Value Strategy

Impute missing Age values based on Pclass and Title medians rather than overall dataset mean to preserve class-based distribution.

Business Value Delivered

Provides a clean DAX measure foundation for Power BI reporting dashboard, enabling drill-through capabilities across age cohorts and passenger classes.
